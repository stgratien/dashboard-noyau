# Règles du noyau

Consignes techniques valables pour tous les dashboards (extraites du dépôt d'origine ; celles qui citent un client en ont été retirées).

- Use social-totals real blended aggregators for all live social summary views so Global and platform totals cannot diverge; keep unavailable metrics nullable.
- Keep demo publication media and permalinks in the publication data model; local publication detail routes provide honest demo destinations until real links are connected.
- Read social metrics only from Cloud through social-data.functions; Windsor sync is server-only and account-allowlisted in src/client/config.server.ts (read through src/lib/windsor-accounts.server.ts, never imported by browser code); keep Google Analytics empty until its source is connected; never generate website demonstration data.
- Keep inclusive ISO-date period logic in dashboard-periods and the edition list in src/client/config.ts (events.editions, exposed as EDITIONS); exact configured predecessor dates avoid timezone and weekday shifts.
- Generate demo series deterministically by calendar day and follower snapshots by period end; overlapping ranges must agree and all tabs share one period/comparison state.
- Global computes each network's realBlendedTotals once and passes the same objects to its blocks and realAllNetworkTotals; additive headline totals match the blocks, averages use summed numerators/denominators, and ad counts union source-namespaced identities across networks.
- Store social publication thumbnails in private Cloud storage and return short-lived signed read URLs; never display expiring provider URLs.
- Store ad delivery independently at source/platform/ad/day grain with explicit source/day coverage; counts require full period coverage, and Global unions namespaced ad identities to deduplicate cross-platform Meta delivery.
- Derive additional paid cost ratios in social-totals from stored paid metrics, never from blended engagement or views (exception: the CPE, which divides the paid budget by the total, organic + paid, interactions: see the CPE bullet).
- TikTok organic aggregates use deduplicated published-video cohorts with lifetime metrics and explicit publication coverage; never use account daily views/interactions for these totals.
- TikTok totals are the published-video cohort alone: a video's own views and interactions already include the delivery of its promotion (the ads are boosted posts, « Spark Ads »), so realBlendedTotals never adds TikTok Ads impressions, video views, likes, comments or shares to the cohort (tiktokPromoted in social-totals.ts); adding them counted the promoted delivery twice. The paid figures stay the base of the budget and of the costs (CPM = budget ÷ paid impressions, CPV = budget ÷ paid video views). Without known published videos (postsAvailable missing) TikTok impressions, video views and interactions are unavailable, never the paid figure alone; with known publications and no video in the period they read 0, and the network tab then shows no change percentage and the note « Aucune vidéo publiée sur la période » (noTikTokVideo in platform-view.tsx), because the delivery of older videos is not in the period's cohort. Global CPE: TikTok's budget follows the delivery days while its interactions are those of the videos published in the period, so a TikTok with a budget and no interactions is left out of the Global CPE and the total is flagged partial (skip in realAllNetworkTotals). Limits: ads that are not boosted posts (no organic video behind them), ads whose video is not stored in social_posts, and a client with TikTok Ads but no organic TikTok source are not covered by the cohort (their budget stays in the budget and the CPM; the impressions and interactions are then missing or unavailable). On TikTok « Impressions totales » and « Vues de vidéo » are the same figure.
- Follower reads include the latest snapshot at or before each period end; current-only provider snapshots are stored on the actual capture day, with historical estimates reconstructed only across known daily growth.
- Preserve shortcut comparison semantics in shared period state; free calendar apply clears shortcut metadata so identical dates can still compare differently.
- Global partial totals carry metric-specific missing-network lists; averages use matched numerator/denominator coverage and deltas require matching coverage.
- Keep manual follower records separate from provider facts; merge per-day nullable fields in social-followers so synchronization cannot overwrite manual history. Version one-time imports as idempotent seed SQL.
- Compute Global paid cost ratios from paired stored paid numerator/denominator fields, never average platform ratios (the Global CPE too: Σ budget ÷ Σ blended interactions of the same networks, YouTube excluded like its budget).
- Normalize organic account zero runs and merge nullable agency exports in social-organic-history before aggregation; store them separately with versioned idempotent seeds so provider synchronization cannot erase historical corrections or duplicate paid delivery. Use seven consecutive calendar days as the zero-run threshold; account-only unavailability never changes TikTok video cohorts.
- Organic history precedence: mergeOrganicHistory fills only the empty or non-positive provider days by default; `client.data.manualOrganicFirst` (optional list among facebook and linkedin, default none: Instagram and TikTok have their own logic) makes the manual history of those networks come BEFORE the provider values for the days it covers (a blank manual field never replaces a provider value). Choose it when the account export is more complete than the connector. The post count (`manualPostsPublished`) falls back to the stored posts for any period that includes a day without a manual row (all-or-nothing sum), and a period straddling the last covered day mixes export and provider days.
- Propagate source-specific partial coverage through blended platform and Global totals and compare only matching scopes so missing organic history cannot masquerade as performance change.
- KPI/chart definitions live only in src/lib/kpi-definitions.ts (no hover tooltips; used for documentation), so wording stays consistent; the history texts specific to this client (agency exports, stories, Instagram interactions, LinkedIn new-follower limit, publications origin) are kpiNotes in src/client/config.ts (history, followersFallback, newFollowersLinkedin, posts, storyHistory, stories, instagramInteractions: all optional, without them the definitions name no agency, export or date).
- Kpi cards (common.tsx) never show the « partiel » note next to « Donnée non disponible » (a figure that is not shown cannot be partial; other notes stay); the 404 and error pages (src/routes/__root.tsx, src/lib/error-page.ts) are written in French.
- Per-post budget/CPM comes from social_post_ad_daily (ad × post × day linkage), never from account totals; audience demographics are current snapshots in social_audience, replaced per dimension only when the provider returns rows.
- Theme is a .dark class on <html> set before paint from stored choice or system preference; elements marked data-export-hide are excluded from the report image captures and the PDF.
- Google Ads arrives only via POST /api/public/google-ads-ingest (nightly Google Ads Script, Bearer GOOGLE_ADS_INGEST_SECRET, constant-time compare); google_ads_daily is server-only (no browser grants) and is read through google-ads.functions; it is never added to social "Tous réseaux" totals (the combined budget card was removed from Global on 17/10). Source is unavailable after 48 h without data.
- YouTube arrives only via POST /api/public/youtube-ingest (nightly Apps Script, Bearer YOUTUBE_INGEST_SECRET, shared constant-time bearer-auth); youtube_* tables are server-only and read through youtube.functions. Paid YouTube = Google Ads VIDEO/DEMAND_GEN rows; YouTube joins "Tous réseaux" only for subscribers, posts, video views and interactions (never budget/impressions) to avoid double counting with the Google Ads row.
- Instagram: Windsor account metrics (views, likes, comments, shares) already include paid delivery, so Meta Ads impressions are never added; Meta Ads likes/comments/shares are added only on days filled from the agency history, where impressions = organic_views + paid_views of the export. "Vues de vidéo" is the sum of the views (social_posts.metrics.impressions = media_views) of the reels and videos published in the period, never the account views: mergeOrganicHistory does not copy them into videoViews.
- Post formats come from mapPost: Instagram media_type REELS/VIDEO/REEL = Vidéo; Facebook post types photo / album / video_inline / video_direct_response, with /reel/ and /videos/ permalinks, cover_photo = Autre; LinkedIn share_post_type (multiImage = Carrousel / Album) with share_thumbnail as the thumbnail fallback. Stored rows keep their format until they are re-imported or backfilled (seeds/facebook-format-backfill.sql; LinkedIn multiImage rows stored as Autre included).
- Thumbnails (windsor-sync.server.ts): mapPost lists every image address the connector provides (remoteThumbnails: media_thumbnail_url, share_image_url, share_thumbnail, in that order) and the sync stores the first one that downloads (allow-listed hosts, https, image content type, no redirect, under 10 MB); a post that ends without a thumbnail is logged ("Vignette absente", reason without the signed URL) and listed in the sync result (thumbnailGapCount, thumbnailGaps, at most 12 entries), which the cron response keeps in net._http_response: read it to learn why a visual does not show (no address supplied by the connector, host refused, HTTP status, redirect). Windsor limits: Meta organic history is available for 2 rolling years only (date_from >= today minus 2 years, inclusive, and a request that straddles the limit fails as a whole); provider image URLs are signed and expire, so thumbnails are downloaded at sync time and a re-import must run before they expire.
- Google Analytics stays hidden (code kept). The YouTube tab and YouTube's part of "Tous réseaux" appear only once youtube.lastSuccess exists; until then Global does not list YouTube as a missing network.
- Number format: K, M and € stick to the number ("162K", "1,4K€"); no decimal from 100K, one decimal below; millions always keep one decimal ("1,0M"); percentages have no decimal from 10, one below, and no space before %. The header alerts when the oldest Windsor source sync is older than 30 h (sync-staleness.ts).
- .prettierrc says printWidth 100 but the Lovable editor formats at 120: do not run a global prettier/format (huge diff) and do not use lint as a regression criterion; keep diffs minimal.
- CPE (KPI card, Global and last column of the Performance des publications table; PostStats keeps `cpm` for the Top CPM card and the Top post cards, the table sorts on `cpe`): budget ÷ TOTAL interactions, organic + paid (likes + comments + shares, the figure of the Interactions card), the same definition on every network and for every client. Card and Global: realBlendedTotals `cpe` = spend ÷ interactions (Global: Σ spend ÷ Σ interactions through the ratio helper), flagged partial when the interactions are (missing['cpe'], read by the network card through coverageDetails / comparableDelta, by Global and by the report), and unavailable without budget (never « 0,00 € »). Table: the budget of a post's ads (social_post_ad_daily) ÷ the post's own likes + comments + shares as shown in the table, empty when the budget or one of the three figures is unknown or the sum is 0; ad-only « Publicité » rows use the ad's own spend ÷ its interactions. An earlier version divided by the ads' own engagements: dropped, because the ads of some networks (LinkedIn) collect almost only clicks (a few likes for a thousand euros), which made the CPE meaninglessly high.
- A publication whose interactions exceed its impressions has UNKNOWN impressions (postStats: impressions null, shown « — », no engagement rate, outside the visibility rankings and the flops): the network no longer provides its statistics (LinkedIn keeps per-post statistics only about 14 months), and a publication's engagement rate above 100 % is never shown (even the known part of its interactions is enough to trigger it; ad-only « Publicité » rows are not concerned). Unknown impressions and rate show « — » in the table and the Top post cards, the Visibilité card explains itself when every post of the period is concerned, and posts without a sort value are ordered by impressions, interactions, then newest. Its likes, comments and shares (lifetime counts) stay. Month rule (unusableStatMonths in top-posts.ts, read by loadStatGaps in social-data.server.ts over ALL posts so a month looks the same whatever the period shown, never blocking: a failed read sets no month aside and the result is not cached (`statGapsFailed`, like `storiesFailed`), result `unusableStatMonths`, `postStats(x, set)`): a network's month is flagged when it ended at least a year ago (STAT_GAP_MIN_AGE_DAYS: a recent month whose figures have not arrived yet is never set aside) and at least 2 posts and 25 % (inclusive) of its posts have impossible impressions, and then EVERY post of that month has unknown impressions, because a post that still looks plausible (37 impressions, 78 % rate) in a month the network lost cannot be told apart otherwise. Never pass postStats straight to `.map` (the index would be read as the set). Facebook, Instagram and TikTok posts never trigger it.
- Budgets use formatBudget (whole euros below 1,000, K with a comma above: 8€, 1,5K€, 162K€; a real budget under 0.50 EUR reads "<1€"); other euro figures keep formatEuro.
- Platform tab layout: the audience chart is full width, then "Publications par format" and "Répartition des interactions" share one line; "Activité" has a "Vidéos publiées" card on every network tab except TikTok (its "Vidéos publiées" card is already the publications card) and Global has one too. The stored format "Carrousel / Album" is displayed per network by formatLabel ("Album" on Facebook, "Carrousel" elsewhere, never both). Every chart axis uses axisNumber (thousands separated by a space).
- Top publications format filter: a "Format" button group beside the "Type" group on the same row (wrapping cleanly on phones; the rate column header reads "Taux d’engagement" on every network, never wrapped), shown only when two or more formats are present; formats come from the table rows, normalized by postFormat: reels, videos and every TikTok post = "Vidéo", unknown "Post" = "Non disponible", carousel per network by formatLabel, ad rows = "Publicité". It composes with the type filter, sorting and the pages (both filters and every sort reset to page 1), falls back to "Tous" when the selected format is absent from the period, is data-export-hide, and drives the table only: the Top posts and Flops cards, the report images and the exports never change with it. Empty result: "Aucune publication pour ces filtres".
- Flops (network tabs only, section after Top posts; Global has no per-post ranking, so no Flops): flopPosts in top-posts.ts ranks the account's own publications of the period (never ad-only rows, never older promoted posts; a boosted account post stays in and is marked Paid) per criterion, weakest first: Visibilité = fewest impressions (views on TikTok), Engagement = lowest engagement rate. A publication is ranked only when published at least FLOP_MIN_AGE_DAYS = 3 days (72 h) before now, and when its criterion figure is known and non-zero (a 0 means not reported; the engagement rate also needs likes, comments and shares). At most FLOP_COUNT = 3 per criterion and never more than half of the comparable publications; fewer than 2 comparable = no ranking ("Pas assez de publications comparables sur la période (3 jours minimum après publication, chiffres connus)"; no post at all = "Aucune publication sur la période"). Ties: the other figure, then the oldest. Ranked list per card (rank, thumbnail, linked title through PostLink/safePostUrl, date · format · Paid, figures under the title so phones stay readable) rather than single cards: a single worst post says little, three give something to review. Definitions: flopVisibility / flopEngagement in kpi-definitions.ts. Flops are not captured for the Google Slides report (no reportKey, no template slot).
- TikTok: the Top publications column is "Vues" (video views, not impressions) and rows are ranked by it; a TikTok ad row has no views and ranks last. The Top post cards for visibility and engagement fall back to the best older promoted post when no post of the period can be ranked, and say "Aucune publication sur la période" when there is none at all.
- loadSocialData reads its independent tables in parallel and keeps each result 2 minutes in memory (SOCIAL_DATA_CACHE_MS, 0 disables); the Performance des publications columns sort on click and the table lists every row of the period, 10 per page (TABLE_PAGE_SIZE and pageWindow in top-posts.ts; "Précédent / 1 2 … 27 / Suivant" and "Publications 11–20 sur 266" under the table, data-export-hide, shown only above 10 rows; changing page brings the section title back into view when it has scrolled out; the page never exceeds the last one when the period changes); card titles go singular for 0 and 1 (labelForValue); videosNote no longer repeats the video count (own card).
- Dashboard UI layout of the code: src/routes/index.tsx only keeps the route, the header, the tabs and their queries; the views live in src/components/dashboard/ (common: shared cards, panels, formats, axis helpers; platform-view: network tabs and Top publications; overview: Global; google-ads-view, youtube-view and website-view, which load on demand with React.lazy inside Suspense). Add new shared pieces to common.tsx, never import a view from another view.
- Known limits of the native link: an ad made from an Instagram post files its Facebook-placement rows under the Instagram media id, so when that post is not stored the Paid row on the Facebook tab has no link (the dark page post it used to point to was visible to page admins only); the days of an ad not yet re-imported (old media-copy ids) stay Paid rows and can still be attached by the name rule (paidOnlyAds judges row by row). The two audience tables and social_ad_post_links are optional: the code works without them. "Actualiser" bypasses the 2-minute server cache (fresh flag); the Performance des publications table only exports the page on screen (10 rows at most), never captured for the report; the lazy tabs sit inside TabBoundary (a missing chunk reloads the page once, then shows an inline message).
- CI: the workflow is kept in docs/github-actions-ci.yml (type check, tests, build on every push to main and every pull request). The GitHub token used for pushes has no `workflow` scope, so GitHub refuses a file under .github/workflows: to enable it, copy the file to .github/workflows/ci.yml from the GitHub web interface, or push it with a token that has the `workflow` scope.
- Header and tabs: the four header buttons (period, comparison, Actualiser, theme toggle) share one row, vertically centred on the logo/title block; on phones the theme toggle moves up beside the period button (`order`). From the `lg` breakpoint the tabs are a left menu (sticky under the header, whose height is measured with a ResizeObserver); below `lg`, or with `?menu=haut`, they stay on a row under the header. The side menu is a test: dropping the `sideMenu` branch in routes/index.tsx restores the old layout.
- Report image capture (report-capture.tsx): the off-screen host is 1352 px wide, so every layout class of a captured block switches on the `ReportRowLimit` context, never on a viewport breakpoint (lg:/sm:); lazy images of the captured blocks (`[data-report-image] img`; the Performance table and Flops thumbnails are never captured, so they are not awaited) are forced eager, with an 8 s wait per image and a 20 s limit per capture, so nothing can freeze the "images" step; gender and age panels are never cut, ranking panels are cut to REPORT_ROWS and their title says "top N"; an empty panel is not tagged (no image, its template slot is removed). A cached Google token is reused only with 15 min left (the image phase takes minutes); an image slot missing from the chosen template is reported in `imageNotes`, shown under "Images du dashboard".
- LinkedIn post text arrives with the connector's markup (hashtags `{hashtag|\#|tag}`, mentions `@[Name](urn:li:...)`, backslash escapes): cleanPostText (top-posts.ts) cleans it when titles are built, for display only (stored titles stay raw, other networks are untouched).
